Job Description

This is a remote position.

The English Senior Tutor at SEHYOG Academy is responsible for delivering high-quality English instruction across the UK curriculum (Key Stage 2 to A-Levels), including English Grammar, Creative Writing, English Language, English Literature etc. The role combines teaching excellence with academic leadership, involving mentoring junior tutors, monitoring student progress, developing curriculum-aligned resources, and maintaining strong communication with parents. The ideal candidate has extensive UK curriculum teaching experience, strong leadership skills, and a commitment to academic excellence.

Requirements

  • Strong command of English Language, Literature, and Creative Writing.
  • Proven experience teaching GCSE and other UK curriculum standards.
  • Excellent communication skills.
  • Strong classroom and behaviour management.
  • Ability to lead and mentor junior tutors.
  • Creative, organized, and committed to academic excellence.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary aligned with experience and expertise
  • Opportunity for academic leadership and career progression
  • Professional development through training, workshops, and curriculum exposure
  • Leadership role with mentoring and decision-making responsibilities
  • Collaborative and supportive academic environment
  • Access to high-quality teaching resources and structured curriculum support
  • Performance-based incentives and recognition
  • Flexible teaching schedules (where applicable)
  • Long-term association with a growing academic institution
  • Opportunity to make a meaningful impact on student outcomes
